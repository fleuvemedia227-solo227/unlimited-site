Frequently asked questions

How much does a website or app cost?

The price depends on the size of the project: number of pages, features, mobile app. After a free first conversation, we send you a detailed quote.

How long does it take to build my website?

A business website takes a few weeks; a custom app takes longer. The schedule is agreed with you in the quote.

Where do you install GPS trackers?

Our technicians come to install the tracker on your vehicle or machine, at the agreed place and date. Contact us for covered areas.

Is industrial equipment delivered?

Yes, we can deliver equipment to your site and commission it. Conditions are set out in the quote.

Do you offer artificial intelligence?

Yes: an assistant that answers your customers on your website, automation of repetitive tasks, data analysis and team training. We start from your real needs and explain everything simply.

I don't have any digital tools yet: where do I start?

With a free first conversation. We look at your business together and suggest a simple first step, such as a business website or an invoicing tool, before going further.

Do you provide maintenance?

Yes: maintenance of websites and apps, GPS tracker support and equipment servicing, depending on the chosen contract.
